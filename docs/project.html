ECON 5371

Forecasting the college NIL market

Extending prior panel-data research with time series methods

Overview

I am developing a time series forecasting project on the college name, image, and likeness (NIL) market as part of ECON 5371. The project extends research I completed in a previous panel-data course and shifts the focus from explaining differences across athletes, schools, or markets to studying how NIL activity changes over time.

Research direction

The project will organize available NIL measures into a time series and evaluate their trend, persistence, and forecastability. Depending on data availability and frequency, potential outcomes include NIL valuations, deal activity, or aggregate market estimates.

Planned approach

  1. Recover and document the prior NIL dataset and model.
  2. Identify a consistent time-indexed NIL outcome and relevant explanatory variables.
  3. Visualize the series and evaluate stationarity, trend, seasonality, and structural breaks.
  4. Estimate appropriate benchmark and stationary time series models.
  5. Compare out-of-sample forecast performance and interpret the economic implications.

Status

Proposal stage. The research question and prior panel-data foundation are established. The next step is to retrieve the earlier project files from OneDrive, assess the available time coverage, and finalize the outcome to be forecast.
